# Placas de Siembra: walkthrough video plan

**What it is:** A landing page for custom seed-plate (placas de siembra) makers. Farmers pick their planter, seed and plate type, and the page writes the quote request for them as a WhatsApp message.
**For:** Argentine farmers and contractors who need the right plate for their sembradora.
**The hook:** The site's own headline, *"La placa justa para cada semilla que sembrás."*, animating in over the real hero.
**The angle:** The form at the bottom is the product. You tap chips, the message **writes itself** in the green panel, and one tap sends the quote request.
**Tone:** default. Punchy, clean and warm, in the site's own palette.
**Identity:** green #1d5628, cream #f6f2e8, yellow #f6e81d, wine #541d1d. Bitter 800 for headings, Figtree for body text.
**Share caption:** "Pedir presupuesto de placas de siembra ahora es tocar opciones: el mensaje se arma solo y sale por WhatsApp."

## Storyboard (22s, 120 BPM, 11 bars; clicks land on the 8th-note grid)

| # | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3.5 | Hook | The real hero builds in (headline, image and "Ejemplo de pedido" badge). The cursor enters and clicks **Armar mi pedido**. |
| 2 | 3.5–7.5 | Walkthrough | The page pulls back into a browser frame and scrolls to the plate types. Caption: *Redondas y alargadas, a medida.* Then it scrolls to the form. |
| 3 | 7.5–14.75 | **The form** | Caption: *Elegí tu máquina y tu semilla*. Clicks: Crucianelli → Soja → Redonda → 2 hileras → 60 alvéolos. Caption: *Tu mensaje se arma solo*. It types the size 12×8×5,5 and taps + up to 8 plates. The "Todavía falta" box disappears. |
| 4 | 14.75–18 | Result | The camera zooms into the "Tu mensaje" panel. Caption: *Pedí presupuesto por WhatsApp, en un toque.* Click on **Enviar pedido por WhatsApp**, with a chime. |
| 5 | 18–22 | Outro | Dip to green, then the logo, the headline and a yellow WhatsApp pill with the real number. |

**Sound:** D major at 120 BPM with a pluck arpeggio, a soft pad, bass, a light kick and a shaker. Each form click plays the next note of the D pentatonic scale, so the melody fills up as the form fills up. Typing uses soft ticks, and the send click gets a bell chord.
